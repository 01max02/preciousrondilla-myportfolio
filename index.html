Hello! I'm Precious Rondilla, a fourth-year Bachelor of Science in Information Technology student specializing in Web and Mobile Application Development. Although my specialization focuses on development, I'm particularly interested in exploring opportunities in UI/UX Design, Networking, Data Analytics, and Quality Assurance (QA).
